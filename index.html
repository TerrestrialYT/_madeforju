The Gooey Heat Guide

Get that authentic fresh-out-of-the-bakery texture

Method 1: Conventional Oven / Air Fryer (Recommended)

Preheat to 160°C (320°F). Warm for 3 to 4 minutes. Let it rest for 1 minute so the outer shell regains its crispy bite while the chocolate pool remains gooey.

Method 2: Microwave (Instant Craving)

Place on a microwave-safe plate and heat for 12 to 15 seconds. Enjoy immediately with a cold glass of milk!

Storage & Freezing

Keep in an airtight jar for up to 4 days. You can also freeze for up to 1 month—bake straight from frozen at 160°C for 6–8 minutes.
